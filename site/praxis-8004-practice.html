About this practice test

Every question is original, written to the official Praxis 8004 content category definitions published by ETS — none are recycled official test items. Single-answer questions are graded the moment you pick an option. The three multiple-select items are worded the way the official samples are worded — Which TWO of the following — and are graded as a set when you press Check, because one click cannot be a set.

Every question comes with an explanation for the correct option and a separate explanation for each of the other options. Social studies distractors are usually adjacent facts: the right answer to a slightly different question. Knowing why the adjacent fact is wrong here is the point of the exercise, so a verdict of “incorrect” with no reason would defeat it.

The bank is machine-drafted and has not been through human fact-checking yet. Every item is flagged draft until it is. If one of them is wrong, tell us — we correct errors and note the correction.
