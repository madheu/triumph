Praxis 5001 Requirements in Kentucky: Tests, Scores, and How to Get Certified

2026-08-24 · Reading time: about 5 min

Kentucky is one of the few states where the Praxis 5001 — Elementary Education: Multiple Subjects — remains the listed content test for elementary (P–5) certification. Kentucky's administrative regulation 16 KAR 6:010 names the 5001 for the elementary certificate, with qualifying scores set in state rule rather than left to a prep-company summary.[1] While several states have moved elementary candidates toward the 7001 series, the CKT series, or the new 8000-series tests, Kentucky candidates can prepare for a stable, well-defined target.

Kentucky's Elementary Tests at a Glance

Under 16 KAR 6:010 §2(2) and ETS's Kentucky requirements page, the elementary (P–5) content requirement is:[1][2]

Elementary Education: Multiple Subjects5001$180Combined: see subtests
Reading and Language Arts Subtest5002$64157
Mathematics Subtest5003$64157
Social Studies Subtest5004$64155
Science Subtest5005$64159

You can register for the combined 5001 (all four subtests in one session) or take subtests individually. Each subtest is its own gate — there is no averaging across the four.

The Pedagogy Requirement: PLT K–6

Kentucky's regulation also lists a pedagogical assessment: the Principles of Learning and Teaching (PLT): Grades K–6 (5622), qualifying score 160, fee $156.[1] Confirm whether PLT applies to your pathway with your preparation program and EPSB.

Before the Content Test: Praxis Core for Program Admission

Kentucky lists the Praxis Core Academic Skills tests as an admission requirement for educator preparation programs (not a licensure exam):[1]

Core testCodeQualifying score (admission)
Core Academic Skills: Reading5713150
Core Academic Skills: Writing5723158
Core Academic Skills: Mathematics5733144

If you are already admitted to a program, you likely cleared this step. If you are applying now, note that admission lines differ from the licensure lines above.

How Scoring Works in Kentucky

The numbers that matter for licensure are the 5001 subtest lines: 157 / 157 / 155 / 159.[1] A strong math score does not offset a below-157 reading score — you need every gate. Kentucky also applies a five-year validity window to accepted scores, so a passing score from more than five years ago may no longer count toward your current application.[1]

These scores are set in Kentucky regulation and can be updated. Treat this page as a snapshot as of August 2026, and confirm current numbers with the Kentucky Education Professional Standards Board and ETS's Kentucky page before registering.

What Else Goes Into a Kentucky Elementary License

Praxis scores are part of the Kentucky licensure picture, but the full path typically includes:

  1. Completing an approved educator preparation program (or an approved alternative pathway).
  2. Meeting the assessment requirements in 16 KAR 6:010 — the Praxis 5001 family for elementary content, plus PLT K–6 where required.[1]
  3. Passing the required Praxis tests within the score-validity window.
  4. Applying through the Kentucky Education Professional Standards Board with official score reports.

Your preparation program is a second source of truth: programs often impose internal deadlines and score floors stricter than the state minimum.

Is Kentucky Switching to a Newer Test?

As of August 2026, Kentucky's regulation and ETS's Kentucky page continue to list the 5001 family, with no adoption date announced for the 8000 series.[1][2] But ETS launched the 8000-series Elementary Education Fundamentals tests in spring 2026, and states adopt new series at their own pace.[3] If you are planning to test several months out, re-check whether Kentucky has updated its accepted codes — the 8000-series blueprints differ from the 5001 in question counts and category weights.

How to Confirm Your Requirement (Do This Before Registering)

  1. Read 16 KAR 6:010 — Kentucky's administrative regulation listing required tests and scores by certification area.
  2. Open the ETS Kentucky requirements page for current fees and registration details.
  3. Cross-check with the Kentucky Education Professional Standards Board for the official qualifying scores and any recent changes.
  4. Ask your educator preparation program about internal deadlines and score requirements.
  5. Re-check before you register — accepted codes and scores can change.

Frequently Asked Questions

What Praxis test do I need to be an elementary teacher in Kentucky?

Per 16 KAR 6:010 §2(2), elementary (P–5) candidates take Elementary Education: Multiple Subjects (5001) — the combined exam or its subtests 5002–5005 — with PLT K–6 (5622) listed as the pedagogy assessment.[1]

What is the passing score for Praxis 5001 in Kentucky?

Kentucky sets 157 for 5002, 157 for 5003, 155 for 5004, and 159 for 5005; PLT K–6 requires 160. Confirm current numbers with the Kentucky Education Professional Standards Board before registering.[1]

Do Kentucky Praxis scores expire?

Yes — Kentucky applies a five-year validity window to accepted scores. A passing score older than five years may not satisfy your current application; check EPSB guidance for details.[1]

Can I take the 5001 subtests separately in Kentucky?

ETS lists the individual subtests ($64 each) as an option alongside the combined 5001 ($180), so subtest-by-subtest registration is available where Kentucky's requirements allow it. Confirm with EPSB and your program before registering.[2]

Is Kentucky switching to the 8000 series?

No switch is announced as of August 2026 — Kentucky still lists the 5001 family. Re-check ETS and EPSB if you plan to test months out, since states are adopting the 8000 series at their own pace.[1][3]

The Bottom Line

Kentucky still requires the Praxis 5001 for elementary (P–5) certification — subtest lines of 157/157/155/159, plus PLT K–6 at 160 and a five-year score-validity window. Confirm the current rules with the Kentucky Education Professional Standards Board and ETS before you register, and re-check if you plan to test after a new test series is adopted.
