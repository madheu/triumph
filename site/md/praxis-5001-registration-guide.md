# How to Register for the Praxis 5001: Cost, Scheduling, and What to Do First

*2026-08-24 · Reading time: about 6 min*

date: "2026-08-24"

Registering for the Praxis 5001 is a four-step online process through your ETS Praxis account: find your tests, schedule a date and location, review your cart, and pay.[1] Before you start, there is one step that saves more money and time than anything else: **confirm your state still requires the 5001** — and which subtests you need to pass. With the new 8000-series Elementary Education Fundamentals tests rolling out, registering for the wrong code is the most expensive mistake a candidate can make.[2]

## Before You Register: Confirm Your Test Code

Praxis 5001 is the combined code for four subtests — 5002 Reading and Language Arts, 5003 Mathematics, 5004 Social Studies, and 5005 Science.[3] Some states still require the 5001 family; others have moved to the 7001 series, the 7811 CKT series, or the new 8000 series. Your state's licensing agency decides which code and qualifying score apply to you, not ETS and not a prep website.

Check your state on the [ETS state requirements directory](https://praxis.ets.org/state-requirements.html) before registering. Then confirm the details with your licensing agency or educator preparation program.

## How Much Does the Praxis 5001 Cost?

As of August 2026, the ETS store lists:

| Item | Fee |
| --- | --- |
| Praxis 5001 combined (four subtests in one session) | $180 |
| Individual subtest (5002, 5003, 5004, or 5005) | $64 each |
| Register by phone (additional surcharge) | $35 |
| Rescheduling fee | $40 (per ETS rescheduling policy) |

Sources: ETS product pages for 5001 and subtests;[3][4][5] ETS rescheduling policy.[6] Fees change — confirm current amounts on the ETS Praxis store before checkout.

Two things are included when you register, so do not pay extra for them elsewhere: you can send your scores to up to **4 agencies or institutions for free**, and you receive a **free Interactive Practice Test** for the test you register for.[1]

## The Four Registration Steps (Online)

### Step 1: Find your test(s)

Sign in to your ETS Praxis account (or create one). Search for "Elementary Education: Multiple Subjects (5001)" or add the individual subtests 5002–5005 to your cart. If your state allows individual subtests, decide whether to take the combined 5001 or register subtest by subtest — the combined option is a single $180 appointment; separate subtests are $64 each.[3][4]

Review the [test availability PDF](https://praxis.ets.org/on/demandware.static/-/Library-Sites-ets-praxisLibrary/default/dwcdd6b0cb/pdfs/Test-Availability.pdf) to see which test centers and dates are open near you.

### Step 2: Schedule your test and location

Choose to test **at home** (where available) or at a **test center**, then pick a date and time slot.[1] At-home testing has its own rules — equipment checks, a quiet room, and a proctoring session — so read the [at-home testing guide](https://praxis.ets.org/test-takers/test-day-at-home.html) if you go that route. Test-center testing follows the standard [test-day rules](https://praxis.ets.org/test-takers/test-day-test-center.html).

After scheduling, you choose your educator preparation program (EPP), select up to four free score recipients, and get your free Interactive Practice Test.

### Step 3: Review your cart

Check your personal information, add any test-prep material you want (optional), and enter any promo or voucher code at checkout.[1]

### Step 4: Enter your payment information

Pay by credit/debit card or another available method, then confirm the amount due. Once paid, your appointment is scheduled and your free score sends are locked in.[1]

## Alternative: Register by Phone

If you cannot register online, ETS accepts phone registration:

- You must have a Praxis account and test at a U.S. test center.
- There is a **$35 surcharge**.
- Call **1-800-772-9476** (Monday–Friday, 8:00 AM–7:45 PM U.S. Eastern Time) at least four days before your test date, paying by credit/debit card or PayPal.[1]

Phone registration is a fallback, not the default — online registration is faster, cheaper, and shows you real-time availability.

## After You Register

Three things happen once your registration is complete:

1. **You get a free Interactive Practice Test** for your registered test — use it, since it mirrors the actual testing interface.
2. **Your free score sends are assigned** — up to four agencies or institutions. Verify the recipient codes with your licensing agency before test day.
3. **Your appointment is set** — note the date, time, and location (or at-home session start window).

If your plans change, ETS lets you [reschedule, retake, or cancel](https://praxis.ets.org/manage-praxis-test-appointment.html) through your account — rescheduling typically costs a $40 fee and must be done before the deadline in the [Information Bulletin](https://praxis.ets.org/on/demandware.static/-/Library-Sites-ets-praxisLibrary/default/dw4d1182d7/pdfs/praxis-information-bulletin.pdf).[6]

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much does it cost to take the Praxis 5001?

The combined 5001 appointment is listed at $180 as of August 2026. Individual subtests are $64 each, so four subtests separately cost $256 — the combined option is the cheaper route when your state accepts it.[3][4]

### Can I take the 5001 subtests on different days?

Yes, when your state permits individual-subtest registration. Many candidates take one or two subtests at a time to pace their study. Check your state's rule — some states require all subtests in one sitting.

### How do I find Praxis test dates near me?

After selecting your test in the ETS store, the scheduling step shows available dates and time slots at nearby test centers or at-home sessions. The [test availability PDF](https://praxis.ets.org/on/demandware.static/-/Library-Sites-ets-praxisLibrary/default/dwcdd6b0cb/pdfs/Test-Availability.pdf) lists which tests run on which schedules.[1]

### What ID do I need for test day?

You need valid, unexpired government-issued photo ID matching your registration name exactly. Review the [ID requirements](https://praxis.ets.org/test-takers/test-day-id-requirements.html) page before test day.

### What happens if I need to reschedule?

Log in to your ETS account and use the manage-appointment flow. ETS applies its rescheduling policy (typically a $40 fee) and deadlines from the Information Bulletin — reschedule before the cutoff or you risk forfeiting the fee.[6]

### Should I wait for the 8000 series instead?

Only if your state or licensing agency has already adopted the 8000-series tests for your application window. The 5001 family is still listed and sold by ETS, but adoption is a state decision.[2][3] Do not assume the newest test is the right test — verify your code first.

## The Bottom Line

Registration is a four-step online process: pick your test, schedule, review, pay. The total for the combined 5001 is $180 (or $64 per subtest), phone registration adds a $35 surcharge, and rescheduling costs about $40. Register only after confirming your state's test code — with the 8000-series transition underway, that confirmation is the difference between a $180 step toward certification and a $180 mistake.
