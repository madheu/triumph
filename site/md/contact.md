# Contact Learndiag Support

Learndiag is built and run by one independent developer — there is no call center and no ticketing platform. The working channel is email, and it is read by the person who runs the site. You will normally get a reply **within two business days**, often faster.

If your email client does not open a compose window (some browsers block mailto links), copy the address above and send from your own mail app.

## What to include

If you have an account, write from the email address on it. When reporting a problem, include what you were doing, what you expected, and what you actually saw — a screenshot helps more than a long description. You do not need to include anything else; a support request is three or four lines, not an essay.

## What to write to us about

| Topic | What helps most |
| --- | --- |
| Login, verification codes, or account deletion | Your account email and the exact message shown on screen |
| A question or explanation that looks wrong | Question ID (e.g. `5003-041`), the answer you picked, the answer shown as correct, and why it looks off to you |
| API / MCP / agent integration questions | Endpoint, request body, and the JSON response you received (subject line "API" is a plus) |
| Partnership, press, or bulk access | Who you are and what you are building |
| Data or privacy requests | Send from your account email with subject "Privacy" — see the [privacy policy](https://learndiag.com/privacy) for the deletion process and timing |
| Refund requests | Your account email and the date of the charge — see the refund terms below and in the [terms of service](https://learndiag.com/terms) |

Please do not send passwords, API keys, or full payment card numbers in email. If an API key is involved in a support case, generate a throwaway key for testing instead, or say "key redacted" and refer to it by name only.

## Refunds

Because the diagnostic and most of the question bank are free, you can evaluate the service before paying. If you are not satisfied with a Pro subscription, request a refund within **14 days of the charge** from your account email and it will be reviewed (approved refunds go back through the original payment method). Requests after 14 days, or for billing periods you have used, are handled case by case. The full terms are in the [terms of service](https://learndiag.com/terms).

## Data deletion

Write from your account email with the subject "Privacy" and everything tied to that address — account, study state, API keys — is removed within **30 days**. Details are in the [privacy policy](https://learndiag.com/privacy).

## Why two pages can show different scores

The readiness diagnostic and your practice history are separate views of your work: the diagnostic reports one snapshot from a short set of questions, while your practice dashboard accumulates everything you have answered over time. They are not the same calculation, and neither is an ETS score. If you think the numbers disagree in a way that looks like a bug, include both screenshots and we will look.
