What the diagnostic does  **It samples all four subtests equally** — three questions each from 5002, 5003, 5004 and 5005, so a weak subtest cannot hide behind a strong one. **It reports accuracy per subtest**, with correct/attempted counts and the content categories to review next. **It is free and ungated.** You can see the summary without an account; saving it across devices is optional.  What it will not tell you This is the part worth being precise about, because other sites promise more than the data supports.  **No scaled score.** ETS publishes the 100–200 scaled range for Praxis 5001 but no table converting raw answers into a scaled score. A twelve-question sample could not produce one even if that table existed. **No pass prediction.** Passing lines are set by each state, not by ETS, and differ per subtest. We do not hold a qualifying-score table and we do not guess one. **No "you are X points away from passing."** That claim requires both of the above. Either we invent the conversion or we invent the line, and we do neither.  Official Sources Every exam fact above traces to one of these.
