# Praxis 5001 Requirements in Pennsylvania: What Elementary Candidates Take Instead

*2026-08-24 · Reading time: about 5 min*

Pennsylvania is the state where "which Praxis do I take for elementary?" has the simplest answer: for the main elementary certificate you **don't take Praxis at all**. Pennsylvania's PreK–4 elementary certificate runs on the **Pennsylvania Educator Certification Tests (PECT)** — a separate exam series from Praxis. The ETS Praxis page for Pennsylvania contains **no 5001 requirement** (and no 7001, 8000, or CKT entries either). If you searched "Praxis 5001 Pennsylvania" because a prep site or your program mentioned it, the short version is: 5001 is not the test for Pennsylvania's PK–4 license.[1][2]

## Pennsylvania's Elementary Tests at a Glance

| Certificate area | Test | What applies |
| --- | --- | --- |
| PreK–4 (main elementary license) | **PECT PreK–4** — three modules, administered through pa.nesinc.com, **not Praxis** | The exam for PK–4 certification; has its own registration and fee structure outside ETS |
| Elementary Grades 5–6 (add-on, requires PK–4 first) | **Praxis 5154** (Pennsylvania Grades 4-8 Core Assessment: English Language Arts and Social Studies) — $50 — qualifying 152 | One of the two Praxis tests for the 5–6 add-on |
| Elementary Grades 5–6 (add-on) | **Praxis 5155** (Pennsylvania Grades 4-8 Core Assessment: Mathematics and Science) — $50 — qualifying score as listed on the ETS Pennsylvania page (verify before registering) | The other Praxis test for the 5–6 add-on |

ETS's Pennsylvania page shows the 5154/5155 pair under the Grades 5–6 section, so the only Praxis subject tests that touch Pennsylvania elementary certification are these two — the content split mirrors the 5001's four-gate structure (reading/social studies in 5154, math/science in 5155), but the codes and passing lines are Pennsylvania's own.[1]

## The Main Elementary Path in Pennsylvania Is PECT, Not Praxis

Pennsylvania's Department of Education runs the PreK–4 certification exam through the **PECT program** (pa.nesinc.com). Candidates must pass all three PreK–4 modules to qualify for the certificate. PECT is a separate series from Praxis: different registration, different scoring, different test center process.[2] If your prep program or employer references "the Praxis for PK–4," they almost certainly mean PECT — confirm the series with your program advisor before paying for anything.

## Other Praxis Tests on the Pennsylvania Page That Candidates See

- **Middle Level 4–8**: Pennsylvania Grades 4-8 Core Assessment (5152; subtests 5153, 5154, 5155) — for middle-level certificates, not elementary.
- **Career and Technical Instructional II**: Praxis Core Mathematics (5733), $90, qualifying 142 — offered as the Chapter 354 alternative to the 3.0 GPA program-entrance requirement.
- **Initial PK–12 areas**: Pennsylvania lists Praxis tests by subject area for PK–12 certificates. Some initial certificates use a general content test; others are subject-specific. Check your exact certification area on the ETS page — do not assume a code from a different certificate.[1]

## How to Confirm Your Requirement (Do This Before Registering)

1. **Open the [ETS Pennsylvania requirements page](https://praxis.ets.org/state-requirements/pennsylvania-tests.html)** and find your exact certification area — there is no elementary (5001) section on it.
2. **For PreK–4**, go to the [PECT program site (pa.nesinc.com)](https://www.pa.nesinc.com/TestView.aspx?f=HTML_FRAG/PA006_TestPage.html) and read the PreK–4 module requirements and passing standards.
3. **For the Grades 5–6 add-on**, confirm the current 5154/5155 scores on the ETS page and with the [Pennsylvania Department of Education](https://www.pa.gov/agencies/education/).
4. **Ask your preparation program** which exam series they expect for your certificate: PECT or a Praxis subject test.
5. **Re-check before you register** — Pennsylvania updates its certification testing requirements, and program deadlines can be tighter than state minimums.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Do I need Praxis 5001 for elementary certification in Pennsylvania?

No. Pennsylvania's PreK–4 elementary certificate uses the PECT exams, and the ETS Pennsylvania page lists no 5001 requirement. [1][2]

### What is the elementary test in Pennsylvania?

The **PECT PreK–4 assessment** — three modules, administered through pa.nesinc.com. It is a separate exam series from Praxis, with its own fees and registration.[2]

### I want the Grades 5–6 add-on. What Praxis do I take?

The **Praxis 5154** (English Language Arts and Social Studies) and **Praxis 5155** (Mathematics and Science) pair — both required, each $50, with Pennsylvania's own qualifying scores on the ETS page. The add-on presupposes a PK–4 certificate.[1]

### Does Pennsylvania use the 8000 series or CKT?

Not for elementary as of this page's snapshot: the ETS Pennsylvania page shows no 7001, 8000-series, or 7811 CKT entries for elementary candidates.[1]

## The Bottom Line

Pennsylvania elementary candidates pass the **PECT PreK–4** modules, not Praxis 5001. The only Praxis tests relevant to elementary in Pennsylvania are the **5154 + 5155** pair for the Grades 5–6 add-on.
