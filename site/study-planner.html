How the plan is built

Research on licensure prep keeps landing on the same three rules, so the generator encodes them:

  • 60% of time goes to your weakest gate. You need all four subtests to pass — your weakest one is the highest-return investment.
  • Mixed practice beats blocked practice. Every week includes one mixed review day spanning all four subtests so nothing rots.
  • The last week is for rehearsal, not learning. Final days simulate test conditions and re-run a diagnostic — no new material.

Frequently asked

How many weeks should I plan?

4–8 weeks at 45–90 minutes a day covers most candidates. The generator caps the visible plan at 8 weeks — if your exam is further out, extend the drilling phase and re-run your diagnostic weekly.

What if my exam is very close?

With under 2 weeks, concentrate almost entirely on your weakest gate plus mixed review, and skip anything new. Depth beats coverage when time is short.
