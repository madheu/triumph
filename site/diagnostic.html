Free Praxis 5001 Diagnostic — Get an Estimated Scaled Score in 8 Minutes

The Learndiag Praxis 5001 readiness diagnostic is a free 12-question test that converts your answers into an estimated scaled score for each of the four subtests: Reading & Language Arts (5002), Mathematics (5003), Social Studies (5004), and Science (5005). It draws three questions from each subtest, then compares your estimated scores against the passing lines set by your state to forecast whether you are likely to pass.

Most candidates only find out how far they are from passing after paying for the real exam and waiting weeks for scores. This diagnostic closes that gap in about eight minutes: you leave with a per-subtest score estimate, your weakest gate, and a pass-probability forecast, so you know exactly which subtest deserves your study hours first.

  • A pass-probability forecast — a realistic read on where you stand today, not after months of studying.
  • A personalized study plan — daily tasks that target the categories hurting your score the most.

How it works

The free Praxis 5001 diagnostic in eight minutes

The Praxis 5001 (Elementary Education: Multiple Subjects) is four separately scored subtests — 5002 Reading & Language Arts, 5003 Mathematics, 5004 Social Studies, and 5005 Science — and you must pass all four. Most candidates find out which gate failed them only after the score report arrives. The Learndiag diagnostic replaces that surprise with a number.

We draw 12 original questions at random — 3 from each subtest — and map your answers to an estimated scaled score per gate. You get a pass forecast against your state's passing line and a study plan that spends time where you're weakest, not evenly across all four tests.

  • 5002 Reading & Language Arts — phonics, vocabulary, comprehension, writing instruction.
  • 5003 Mathematics — number sense, algebra, geometry, data, and how to teach it.
  • 5004 Social Studies — US history & government, geography, world history, economics.
  • 5005 Science — earth, life & physical science, plus inquiry and pedagogy.
