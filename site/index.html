Why this exam breaks people

It's not one test. It's four gates, in sequence.

Every subtest has its own passing score. Score high on math and fail science — you don't pass, you retake science (full price, 28 days later). Most candidates discover the weak one only after the score report arrives.

This is not a niche fear. A majority of first-time elementary teacher candidates fail the most widely used licensure exam, and its first-time pass rate sits below the bar exam. Some candidates sit for it eleven times.

How it works

Free practice is the bait. The system is the catch.

01

Practice free, on the real blueprint

A free question bank organized by the four subtests — same categories, same weights as the official Study Companion. No paywall on the questions themselves.

02

Get a readiness score, not a vague percentage

Your answers map to each subtest's categories. Learndiag converts them to an estimated scaled score and tells you where you sit against your state's passing line.

03

A plan built for your weakest gate

Study time goes where the gap is — 60% on the subtest most likely to fail you, not equal effort across all four.

04

Track it like a training program

Progress, retake-readiness, and a study schedule that updates as you improve. No more guessing in the last two weeks.
