## MINOR

### 5002-068 — 键：`Tom told Mike that Tom made a mistake.`
- **判级**：MINOR
- **问题说明**：现解释（"Repeats the name but stays indirect; a direct quotation resolves the ambiguity more cleanly than restating 'Tom.'"）只批评该句"间接、不如直接引语干净"。但这并非该选项未当选的关键原因——此改写实际上把犯错者明确改成了 Tom，答错了对象（题干要求指明 Mike 犯错）。只谈文体会让读者误以为该句只是"不够优雅"。
- **建议改写**：
  > Shifts the blame to the wrong person; naming 'Tom' as the one who erred contradicts the intended meaning that Mike made the mistake.
