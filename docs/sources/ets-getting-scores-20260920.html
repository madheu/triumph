Getting Your Praxis Scores

The date you receive your score depends on which test you take.

When will I know my official Praxis scores?

Your official score report will be posted in your Praxis account (in the Score Reports page) on the score reporting date. This date varies based on test type and delivery method (At home vs Test Center).

If you take an Elementary Education Fundamentals (8002-8005) full test or individual Step between September 1-5, 2026, your score report will be available in your Praxis account after 5 p.m. ET on September 11, 2026. If you take the test or Step after September 6, 2026, your score report will be available within 7 calendar days after your test date.

You can check score reporting dates based on the test you are taking and the day you are testing using the score reporting calendar tool below.

Getting Your Praxis Scores

Select the test you're taking from the dropdown. If you don't see your test, contact customer service.

Test Location
    Result

    Note

    Praxis reports scores on Tuesdays and Fridays. If a holiday falls on a Tuesday or Friday scores will be reported on the following Tuesday or Friday, respectively.

    Your Unofficial Score Report

    Praxis tests that include only selected-response questions will offer you an opportunity to view unofficial scores at the end of your testing session. If an unofficial score is not provided for your selected-response test, it means that further analysis must be conducted before scoring can be completed. This does NOT indicate any problem with the administration of your test and will NOT result in any delay of the reporting of your official score. Tests that include constructed-response questions will not provide an unofficial score because of the time needed for your responses to be rated. 

    Test centers cannot provide printed or emailed copies of unoffical scores.

    At the end of the test session, but before reviewing the unofficial score information, the computer will give you the option to report or cancel your score. If you choose to report the score, it cannot be canceled. If you choose to cancel your score, it will not be reported and it cannot be reinstated on your record. You also will not receive a refund if you choose to cancel your score.

    Where to Find the Estimated Score Reporting Date

    During Registration:

    On the scheduling screen after selecting your test date.

    In Your Account:

    On the Score Reports Page > Find your pending score reports card.


    Important

    Cancelled or held tests won’t show up in the Pending Scores section.

    How to access your scores

    Your official score report awaits - here’s how you’ll receive it:

    • Praxis Account: Your official score report will be posted in your Praxis account (in the Score Reports Page) on the score reporting date. An email will notify you when it’s available.
    • Institutions and Agencies: Your scores will also be sent to the institutions or agencies you selected during registration (up to four choices at no extra cost). They’ll receive the score information.
    • State Reporting: If you tested in an automatic score reporting state, your scores will be sent automatically to the state.

    Online Scores: For scores reported on or after July 21, 2017, you can download them from your account. These scores remain accessible for ten years from the reporting date. We recommend that you save a copy for future reference.

    Official Score Report (Pre-2017): To obtain an official score report for scores reported within the last ten years but before July 21, 2017, order an additional score report. Review the details in the Sending Your Scores section.
