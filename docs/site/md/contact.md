## Before you write — quick answers to the common ones

**"Is my answer keyed to a specific state?"** Passing scores for the Praxis 5001 are set by each state, not by ETS, and they vary by subtest. If you are checking which lines apply to your certificate, start with the [passing scores by state](https://learndiag.com/praxis-5001-passing-score-by-state) page before emailing — it is kept current and links to the official sources for every listed state.

**"Why is my dashboard score different from the diagnostic?"** The diagnostic estimates a scaled score from twelve questions; your dashboard reflects accumulated practice history. Formats are described on the [diagnostic page](https://learndiag.com/diagnostic) and in the [study guide](https://learndiag.com/praxis-5001-study-guide). If the numbers genuinely disagree in a way that looks like a bug, include both screenshots.

**"Can I request a refund?"** Yes. Refund terms are in the [terms of service](https://learndiag.com/terms); send the request from your account email and it is handled directly by the developer.
