How to get the most out of these tools

Start with the free Praxis 5001 diagnostic — twelve questions, about eight minutes — and let it estimate your scaled score on each of the four subtests (5002 Reading & Language Arts, 5003 Mathematics, 5004 Social Studies, 5005 Science). The diagnostic compares those estimates to the qualifying scores set by your state, so you see margin instead of guesswork. Next, open the study planner, enter your test date, and the planner weights your daily hours toward the subtest that is most likely to fail you.

For the daily grind, use the question bank to drill by subtest and content category — every question includes a written explanation, and every miss can feed the AI error analysis. If you learn better with recall, keep your weakest points alive with spaced-repetition memory cards. Pro members can also run the full 40-question mock exam under timed conditions and the weak-gate drill pack.

Never taken a Praxis exam before? The Praxis 5001 registration guide walks through creating an ETS account, choosing a test center, and what to bring on test day.

Bookmark the score calculator for quick raw-to-scaled conversions while you study, and check the passing scores by state page whenever you need the exact lines for 5002–5005.
