5002 · Reading & Language Arts Which text structure organizes information by listing reasons and supporting details about a single topic?
Answer: Description. A descriptive structure presents a topic and then lists characteristics, features, or supporting details. Cause and effect shows relationships between events, while sequence organizes events in time order.
5003 · Mathematics A student explains that 0.5 is greater than 0.35 because 5 is greater than 35. Which representation best addresses this misconception?
Answer: the base-ten blocks. Base-ten blocks make the place value explicit: 0.5 fills 5 of 10 columns while 0.35 fills 3 full columns plus 5 small squares. The digit-count argument reinforces the misconception. Memorization does not build understanding. Fraction conversion without visualization does not address the root confusion.
5005 · Science Students believe that seasons are caused by Earth's distance from the Sun. Which demonstration best corrects this misconception?
Answer: the axis-tilt demonstration. Demonstrating axial tilt with a globe and light source shows how the tilted axis causes direct vs. angled sunlight, creating seasons. Correcting without evidence reinforces the misconception. Measuring at noon only ignores seasonal variation. A circular orbit diagram does not address the actual cause.

Why this exam breaks people

It's not one test. It's four gates, in sequence.

Every subtest has its own passing score. Score high on math and fail science — you don't pass, you retake science (full price, 28 days later). Most candidates discover the weak one only after the score report arrives.

This is not a niche fear. A majority of first-time elementary teacher candidates fail the most widely used licensure exam, and its first-time pass rate sits below the bar exam. Some candidates sit for it eleven times.

FAQ

Before you run the diagnostic. The straight answers.

Is the Praxis 5001 practice test free?

Yes. The full question bank and the 12-question readiness diagnostic are free with no signup required. Logging in at the end of the diagnostic is optional and unlocks your full report and study plan.

What is on the Praxis 5001?

The Praxis 5001 (Elementary Education: Multiple Subjects) is four separately scored subtests: 5002 Reading & Language Arts, 5003 Mathematics, 5004 Social Studies, and 5005 Science. You must pass all four.

How long does the Praxis 5001 diagnostic take?

About eight minutes. The diagnostic draws 12 original questions at random — 3 from each of the four subtests.

Does the diagnostic tell me my weakest subtest?

Yes. It estimates a scaled score for each subtest, compares it to state passing lines, and identifies the gate most likely to fail you so you know where to study.

What score do I need to pass the Praxis 5001?

Passing lines are set by each state, not by ETS, and they differ per subtest — most states require between 139 and 159 on each of the four gates. The Praxis 5001 passing scores by state table lists the exact qualifying scores for 5002, 5003, 5004, and 5005 and tells you which states use the exam.

How is this different from grinding random practice questions?

Volume alone doesn't raise a score when the questions don't match the blueprint. Every Learndiag item is original, tagged by subtest category, and explained — start with the free practice questions. The diagnostic then converts your pattern of answers into an estimated scaled score per subtest, your weakest gate, and a plan that puts study time where the gap is.

How is the estimated score calculated?

Each of the 12 questions maps to a subtest category. Your accuracy per subtest is converted into an estimated scaled score on the 100–200 scale ETS reports, and the gap between that estimate and your state's passing line becomes your readiness signal and study plan.
